    Frequently asked questions

    A few helpful answers.

    What to know about Space Zone’s tools, data, and current product preview.

    Are the Product Tools free to use?

    Yes. The tools on this page run in your browser and do not require an account or paid API key. Create a free account and sign in if you want to save generated results in your private dashboard.

    Can I save the files and results I generate?

    Yes. Sign in, generate a result, and choose “Save to dashboard.” Saved results are private to your account and can be downloaded or deleted from your dashboard.

    Does the AI prompt builder send my information to an AI service?

    No. It creates a prompt in your browser for you to copy. It does not call an AI model or send the prompt to an AI provider.

    Does the website audit tool crawl my website?

    No. It only checks HTML that you paste into the tool. It is a basic on-page checklist, not a full crawl, security scan, or search-ranking test.

    Will FAQ structured data guarantee my page appears in AI answers or rich results?

    No. The GEO/AEO tool generates FAQ structured data from your answers, but search engines and answer platforms decide independently whether and how to use it. Keep the same accurate FAQ content visible on your page.

    Does the marketing tool measure campaign performance?

    No. It creates tagged campaign URLs and a launch checklist. Use your own analytics platform to measure visits, enquiries, sales, or other outcomes.

    Is the 10-day trial active, and will I be charged?

    No. The displayed plans and trial are a preview. User accounts are available, but trial activation and billing are not connected, so submitting your email does not start a trial or authorize a charge.

    What happens when I request a welcome email?

    Your email address is saved so Space Zone can manage the request and attempt to send one welcome email. Delivery depends on the server’s email configuration; the request does not create an account.
